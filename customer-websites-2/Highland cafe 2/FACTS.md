# Highland Cafe — verified source data
Compiled 2026-07-20. Menu section added 2026-07-25.
Anything not listed here must NOT appear on the site as fact.

## Confirmed
- Name: Highland Cafe (Highland Café and Bar)
- Address: 12/7 Lat Phrao Road, Khwaeng Chom Phon, Khet Chatuchak, Bangkok 10900
- Phone: 087-330-8506 / +66 87 330 8506
- Instagram: @highlandcafe_official
- Facebook page name: "Highland Cafe and Bar" (exact URL NOT confirmed)
- Opened May 2019 as Thailand's first cannabis-THEMED cafe (sold no cannabis - illegal)
- 9 June 2022: began sales on decriminalisation day; first dispensary in Thailand to sell openly
- Operated by Highland Network, Thai legalisation advocacy group; runs annual 420 festival
- Founder: Rattapon "Guide" Sanrak. Spokesperson: Chokwan "Kitty" Chopaka
- Two floors. Purple neon, cannabis artwork. Designated smoking area only (not main premises)
- ~19 local strains
- Sells: flower, accessories (pipes/bongs/papers), CBD products, Thai dishes cooked with
  cannabis leaf, craft beer on tap, branded merchandise
- Payment: cash, credit card, PromptPay / Thai QR
- Second branch: Khaosan Road
- Prices seen: Sunset Sherbert (indica) THB450/g; Sugarcane (sativa) THB700/g; 3.5g from THB1,400

## Menu - sourced 2026-07-25, now live on menu.html
Food prices come from the shop's own Wongnai x LINE MAN delivery listing
(business id 449408hA), read 2026-07-25. 7 categories, ~60 dishes.
- PASTA, MUNCHIES, DISHES MENU, APPETIZER, THAI SALAD & SOUP, Berger, plus a
  recommended set. Full item/price list is on menu.html; that page is the record.
- Cannabis-leaf dishes (garlic bread 120, bolognese 180, cheeseburger 200,
  truffle cream + bacon 220) come from the shop's own printed menu book,
  photographed on their Google Maps listing.
- Coffee board (Americano through Matcha, 60-90 hot/iced) read off the
  handwritten board behind the espresso machine in their Google Maps photos.
- Beer: 12 taps, 240-300/glass; Budweiser 150/glass, 280/jug;
  Highland House Witt 5% at 240 is their own beer and appears on every tap
  board photographed. Individual guest taps rotate every few days - the site
  deliberately lists a price band and past breweries, not a fixed tap list.
- Dispensary board: flower 250-800/g, 3.5g 750-2500, joints 150-420,
  10g packages 1200-1900. Changes daily; site lists bands only.

### Menu caveats - CONFIRM WITH CLIENT
- Delivery prices carry a platform markup. Their own printed in-store menu is
  cheaper on several dishes (kaphrao moo 120 in-store vs 150 on delivery).
  menu.html currently shows the DELIVERY figures because that is the only
  complete, current set. Ask the client for the dine-in price list.
- The coffee-board photo predates the 2022 refit (Stone Brewing signage in
  frame), so those prices are the oldest data on the page.

  with the rows around it. Transcribed as read; worth confirming.
